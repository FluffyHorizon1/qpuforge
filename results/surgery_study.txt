4. Decoders on the shots of section 2 (same syndromes for every decoder)
   separate matching    one exact matching per observable, each on its own graph (the decoder of section 2)
   joint matching       one exact matching on one graph whose edges carry a mask of the observables they flip; edge probability from all mechanisms
                        of its detector pair, mask of the most likely class. Mechanisms flipping more than two detectors stay out of the graph (hyperedge_p above)
   union-find           the weighted union-find decoder on the same graphs, separate and joint
   correlated           two passes: union-find on the detectors of the other Pauli type, then joint matching with the weights of the edges that share
                        mechanisms with that correction lowered to their conditional probability
   differs / fixed / broken: shots whose prediction differs from separate matching; shots with some observable wrong under separate matching and none
   under this decoder, and the reverse. decode: mean wall time per shot on one thread (informational, not reproducible)
